About us

A family home

Cabañas Barra isn't a hotel. It's our home, and we want you to feel part of it.

We live in Barra de la Cruz with our daughters, Keny and Sofi. We opened the doors of our home to share what we love most about this place with travelers and surfers: the ocean, the calm and family life.

Here we welcome you as friends, help you find the best waves and share tips for enjoying the Oaxacan coast.

Photo: the town
The place

Barra de la Cruz

A small town on the Oaxacan coast, east of Huatulco, surrounded by nature and known among surfers for its wave.

The community looks after its beach with great pride. We ask the same of you: respect the locals, keep the beach clean and take it easy.
