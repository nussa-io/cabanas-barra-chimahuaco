Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll be happy to help.

Your stay

How do I book a room?

Message us on WhatsApp at +52 958 174 2264 with your dates and how many people are coming. We'll reply with availability and price.

Why aren't there prices on the website?

Prices change with the season and the length of your stay. Message us and we'll send you a personal quote.

What does each room include?

Every room has 2 double beds, air conditioning, a fan and a private bathroom.

How many people fit in a room?

Each room has two double beds. Tell us how many of you are coming and we'll suggest the best option.

Can I cook?

Yes. There's a shared kitchen with a stove, fridge, freezer and everything you need to cook your own meals.

What time can I arrive?

Send us your approximate arrival time and we'll make sure someone is there to welcome you.

Getting here

What's the nearest airport?

Bahías de Huatulco airport (HUX). From there, Barra de la Cruz is to the east along the coastal highway.

How do I get there without a car?

Message us before your trip and we'll explain the best transport options.

Where exactly are you?

You can find us on Google Maps.

Surf

Do I need to know how to surf?

Not at all. We have soft-tops for beginners and lessons on request, plus boards for intermediate and advanced surfers.

How do board rentals work?

Boards are rented by the day, with a discount for multiple days. Message +52 958 122 6791 for prices and availability.

Where do the surf trips go?

To different spots between Huatulco and Salina Cruz. We pick the destination based on swell and weather, so you surf the best waves of the day.

Are surf trips for all levels?

Yes. We choose the spot with your level and the conditions in mind.

Do I have to pay to enter the beach?

The Barra de la Cruz community manages its beach and charges an entrance fee. It helps keep the beach clean and cared for.

What should I bring?

Biodegradable sunscreen, a rash guard, water and lots of stoke. If you don't bring a board, you can rent one from us.

The town

Should I bring cash?

Barra de la Cruz is a small town. We recommend bringing cash from Huatulco.

How can I help take care of the place?

Respect the locals and their customs, don't leave trash on the beach and use biodegradable products in the ocean.

Have another question?

Message us on WhatsApp. We reply personally.
